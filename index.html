A little context

Curiosity is the common thread.

I graduated in computer science and mathematics with a minor in psychology from the University of Copenhagen. Before teaching at high-school level, I was a teaching and research assistant in algorithms and data structures.

Today I teach programming, software development, informatics, technical engineering, mathematics, applied machine learning and data science. I also supervise teachers completing their pedagogy exams.
